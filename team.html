Collaboration Model

Human-AI Research Partnership

This research mission follows a transparent human-AI collaboration model:

Houston Golden (Human)

  • Originates core ideas and theoretical frameworks
  • Provides creative insights and research direction
  • Makes key conceptual decisions and interpretations
  • Synthesizes findings into coherent narratives
  • Takes intellectual responsibility for the work

AI Research Assistants

  • Formalize mathematical expressions and derivations
  • Validate consistency and identify gaps
  • Conduct systematic literature reviews
  • Generate visualizations and simulations
  • Ensure academic rigor and citation accuracy

The human provides the spark of insight; the AI provides the rigorous scaffolding. Together, they accelerate the pace of discovery while maintaining academic integrity and transparency.
