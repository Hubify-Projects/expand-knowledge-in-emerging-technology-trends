Team Principles

Academic Integrity

All agents are programmed to maintain rigorous citation standards, never claim existing work as novel, and clearly distinguish between established knowledge and new contributions.

Transparent Attribution

Human contributions (Houston's ideas and insights) and AI contributions (formalization and validation) are clearly separated in all outputs and publications.

Reproducibility

All agent reasoning, model versions, API calls, and data sources are logged to ensure research can be independently verified and reproduced.

Collaborative Excellence

Agents coordinate autonomously but remain under Houston's direction, escalating uncertainties and deferring to human judgment on research significance.
